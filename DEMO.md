# CivicSnap demo plan (about 2 minutes 30 seconds)

## 0:00–0:20 — Introduce the problem

“Small maintenance problems in shared campus spaces can be easy to miss and hard to follow up on. CivicSnap lets someone report an issue in under a minute and gives facilities staff a clear response workflow.”

## 0:20–1:05 — Report an issue

1. Show the visitor home screen on a phone-sized view.
2. Tap **Report a problem**, then take or upload a photo of a test issue.
3. Choose the campus building and area. Add an optional voice note or typed context if helpful.
4. Tap **Review report** and show Gemini's suggested title, description, category, and priority. Edit one detail to show the reporter stays in control.
5. Submit the report and briefly show its tracking view.

Narration: “A photo is required, while a typed description is optional. Gemini uses the photo and any context the visitor adds to draft a report. The visitor reviews it before sending.”

## 1:05–2:05 — Respond as facilities staff

1. Switch to **Staff dashboard** and choose one of the demo staff profiles.
2. Open the report just submitted.
3. Assign it, start work, and mark it resolved. Show the status and response timeline.

Narration: “Facilities staff can see the same report, update its status, and close the loop so the visitor can follow progress.”

## 2:05–2:30 — Close

“CivicSnap connects a clear, photo-based report with a practical facilities workflow. AI helps organize the report, while people review the details and make the decisions.”

## Before recording

- Confirm the deployed app has `GEMINI_API_KEY`, `SUPABASE_URL`, and `SUPABASE_SECRET_KEY` set.
- Test one photo report end to end, including the staff status updates, before recording.
- Use one of the fictional sample photos in `public/test-images/`; these are test assets, not real campus reports.
- Record at a phone-sized viewport or on a phone. Keep text readable and the video below three minutes.
- If the live AI service is unavailable, use the editable manual draft and describe AI as the intended feature only if it was not actually demonstrated.
